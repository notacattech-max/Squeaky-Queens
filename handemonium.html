Handemonium Independent Contractor Agreement

This Independent Contractor Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into between Handemonium ("Platform") and the individual applying to provide services through the Platform ("Contractor"). By accepting this Agreement, Contractor agrees to the following terms:

1. Independent contractor status

Contractor is an independent contractor and not an employee, partner, agent, or joint venture partner of Handemonium. Contractor is solely responsible for all taxes, insurance, and compliance with applicable laws arising from services performed. Handemonium will not withhold any taxes or provide any employee benefits.

2. Services

Contractor agrees to provide handyman and home repair services to customers booked through the Handemonium platform. Contractor agrees to perform all services in a professional, timely, and workmanlike manner consistent with industry standards.

3. Licensing & insurance

Contractor represents and warrants that they hold all licenses, certifications, and permits required by applicable law to perform the services offered. Contractor agrees to maintain general liability insurance with minimum coverage of $1,000,000 per occurrence and to provide proof of insurance upon request.

4. Platform fees

Handemonium charges a platform fee of 15% on all completed bookings. This fee is deducted from the customer payment before remittance to the Contractor. Contractor agrees to the platform pricing minimums as established and updated by Handemonium from time to time.

5. Conduct & professionalism

Contractor agrees to: (a) arrive on time for all scheduled jobs; (b) treat all customers with respect and professionalism; (c) maintain a clean and safe work environment; (d) communicate promptly with customers and the Platform; (e) not solicit customers to book services outside the Platform for a period of 12 months following any job completed through the Platform.

6. Background check consent

Contractor consents to a background check as a condition of approval on the Platform. Handemonium reserves the right to deny or revoke access based on background check results at its sole discretion.

7. Dispute resolution

Any disputes arising out of or related to this Agreement shall be resolved through binding arbitration in Los Angeles County, California, in accordance with the rules of the American Arbitration Association. California law shall govern this Agreement.

8. Termination

Either party may terminate this Agreement at any time with 7 days written notice. Handemonium may terminate immediately for cause, including but not limited to, customer complaints, safety violations, or breach of this Agreement.

9. Limitation of liability

Handemonium's liability to Contractor shall not exceed the total platform fees paid in the 30 days preceding the claim. Handemonium is not liable for any indirect, incidental, or consequential damages.

10. Entire agreement

This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties and supersedes all prior agreements. Handemonium may update these terms with 14 days notice via email.
